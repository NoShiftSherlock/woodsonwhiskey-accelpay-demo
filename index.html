How this works

This is Woodson Whiskey's own Shopify store with the AccelPay Shopify app installed. The cart and checkout are Shopify. AccelPay handles payments, taxes and routing each order to a licensed retailer.

01Shopify cartShoppers add to cart on this site, in the native Shopify cart. No third-party cart and no redirect.
02Shopify checkoutThe one-page Shopify checkout with Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and card payments by AccelPay.
03Licensed retailerThe shipping address picks a licensed retailer in the buyer's state, which sells and ships the bottle.
04Your customer dataEvery order and customer record lands in Woodson Whiskey's Shopify admin. You own the customer.
